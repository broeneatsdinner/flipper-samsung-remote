# Roadmap

## v0

- Home screen with Power and Simulate Remote.
- Physical remote mode for Samsung navigation.
- Hardcoded IR commands copied from a known-working `.ir` file.

## v1

Add hold behavior:

- Short Up sends TV Up.
- Long/Held Up sends Volume Up repeatedly.
- Short Down sends TV Down.
- Long/Held Down sends Volume Down repeatedly.
- Repeater interval starts conservatively, around 250 ms.
